# User guide — Grant Finder

## Purpose
This page helps you check which grants may be relevant to your business. It is a decision-support tool only and should be used as a guide, not as official financial or legal advice.

## What the page does
You can:
- enter your business profile
- see which grants may match your circumstances
- view plain-language reasons for each result
- filter to eligible grants only
- sort results by amount
- open a placeholder details page for each grant

## Before you start
- Use the information for your business as accurately as you can.
- If you are unsure about a field, enter the best estimate you can.
- The results are based on sample data and are intended for demonstration purposes.

## How to use the page

### 1. Enter your business details
Fill in the form with your business information, including:
- state
- industry
- employee count
- turnover
- years trading

Make sure each required field is completed before you submit.

### 2. Review the results
After you submit, the page will show a list of grants. Each grant will include:
- the grant name
- the grant amount
- whether it appears eligible or ineligible
- a short explanation of why it matched or did not match

### 3. Understand the outcome
Read the explanation for each grant. The wording tells you which rule was met or missed, such as:
- state eligibility
- industry eligibility
- employee limit
- turnover limit
- trading history requirement

### 4. Narrow the results
Use the eligible-only filter if you want to see only the grants that appear to match your profile.

### 5. Compare grants by value
Use the sort option to order grants by amount, starting with the largest opportunities first.

### 6. Open more information
If you want to learn more about a grant, select the details link for that result. The page opens a placeholder details view for the demo.

## Tips
- If no grants appear, check whether your details are complete and whether they fall within the sample eligibility rules.
- If you change one field, submit the form again to refresh the results.
- Keep the page open while you compare a few scenarios.

## Important note
The results are indicative only. They are based on sample data and do not replace official grant guidance or professional advice.
